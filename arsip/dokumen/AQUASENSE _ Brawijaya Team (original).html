We grow lettuce. And an AI that looks after it.

Aquaponics saves water, but it’s hard to keep fish, plants and water chemistry in balance. Most people don’t have an expert on call.

AQUASENSE puts that expert in the system: sensors and a camera feed two local AI “brains” that diagnose problems and explain what to do next. No cloud needed.

The team presented AQUASENSE at the World Young Inventors Exhibition and came home with a Gold Award.

UB students win Gold with an AI that needs no internet

A multidisciplinary Universitas Brawijaya team won a Gold Medal at the World Young Inventors Exhibition 2026 in Kuala Lumpur, part of ITEX. Their entry, AQUASENSE, is an aquaponics monitor whose AI runs entirely on local hardware.

The event drew more than 1,000 inventions from 17 countries. In the final round the team demonstrated the system live and pitched to international judges and industry investors.

Under the hood, AQUASENSE joins IoT sensors, edge computing, YOLOv11 computer vision for leaf disease and lettuce harvest readiness, and a retrieval-augmented AI agent that suggests what to do next.

Three Faculty of Computer Science students, Qudwa Abyaz Ghiffara, Dyandra Aurellia Agata Fitri and Aliya Mutia Nashwa, competed with four teammates from other UB faculties, including Inez Syawalytrie Favourita of the Faculty of Agroindustrial Technology and Biosystems.

Three things go wrong

Complexity. Balancing fish, plants and water takes expert knowledge most users lack.

Inefficiency. Manual checks are slow, error-prone and late.

Separate monitoring. Water and plants are tracked apart, so nobody sees the whole picture.

One system, two brains.

Drag or scroll sideways →

Step 1

Sensors & camera

pH, TDS, water temperature and turbidity, plus a fixed camera that photographs the lettuce canopy on its own. Runs on a low-cost ESP32-S3.

Brain 1

Reflex Vision

YOLO computer vision spots leaf disease and tells you when lettuce is ready to harvest. Fast, local, no internet.

Brain 2

Cognitive Agronomy

Links water readings to plant health, applies safety overrides, and answers questions with RAG over agronomy documents using a local LLM.

Step 3

Dashboard & alerts

Live view of the pipe and tank, history charts, an AI chat, harvest certificates as PDF, and Telegram alerts when something is critical.

Also

Passive oxygenation

Water gets oxygen without extra pumps or power, which keeps maintenance low.
